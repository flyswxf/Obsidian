UNION, INTERSECT, EXCEPT
- 集合间操作
- 都**默认去重**
- 添加后缀**ALL**之后, **保留重复项**
- 操作对象为两个RELATION
```
(select course_id from section where semester = 'Fall' and year= 2017) 
except all 
(select course_id from section where semester = 'Spring' and year= 2018);
```

SOME, ALL
- 集合比较操作
- 结合`where`使用,  如`where salary > some\all (select salary from instructor where dept_name = 'Biology')`
